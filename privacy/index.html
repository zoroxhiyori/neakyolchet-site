Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026 · The English text is the official version.

This policy covers this website and the content studio behind the social channels អ្នកយល់ចិត្ត (Neak Yol Chet, in Khmer) and Bonding Souls (in English), run from Phnom Penh, Cambodia ("we", "us"). Contact: telyhav3@gmail.com.

1. This website

This website has no sign-in, no forms, no comments, no cookies, no advertising and no analytics. We do not collect any personal information from visitors. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

2. How we use YouTube, Meta and TikTok

Our studio software connects to the platforms below only with our own accounts, to run our own channels:

  • YouTube API Services (Google): upload, schedule, edit and delete videos on our own YouTube channels; read and reply to comments on our own videos; read statistics about our own channels (views, watch time, subscribers).
  • Meta Graph API (Facebook and Instagram): publish, edit and delete posts on our own Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts; read, reply to, hide or delete comments on our own posts; read insights about our own Pages and accounts.
  • TikTok (through a posting service): publish our own videos and photo posts and read their statistics.

We do not use these APIs to access anyone else's account, and we never sell, rent or share data from them.

3. Comments and messages on our posts

When you comment on or message one of our channels, the platform shows us what you chose to post publicly or send us: your public profile name and the text. We use it only to read and reply within that platform, to remove spam or abuse, and to count totals (for example, how many comments a post received). Replies may be drafted with the help of AI writing tools and are checked before they are posted. We do not build profiles of people, do not combine this information with other sources, and keep any working copies only as long as needed to reply (at most 90 days). Statistics we keep are totals, not information about individuals.

4. Google and YouTube

By interacting with our YouTube channels you also use YouTube, which is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The only Google account our software connects to is our own; it can be disconnected at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. Deleting your information

You can delete your own comments at any time on the platform where you posted them. To ask us to delete a reply, a comment, a message or any information we hold about you, email telyhav3@gmail.com with a link to the post or a description, and we will act within 30 days. Facebook and Instagram users can also remove our app's access in their Facebook settings under "Apps and websites".

6. Children

Our channels are for adults and families; they are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
